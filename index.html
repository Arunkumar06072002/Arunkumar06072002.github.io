About me

Building software teams can rely on

I am a Software Engineer at Orion Innovation in Coimbatore, where I started as a project intern in January 2022. I specialise in full stack development with the MEAN Stack, and I enjoy the full path of a feature: designing the API, building the Angular screens, testing every endpoint in Postman and debugging until it is solid.

Most of my work has been for the Australian Football League: a contract management system that clubs and players use to create and manage contracts, and a Match Day Portal that tracks player injuries, rounds and seasons. Earlier I automated project monitoring in Microsoft Azure with PowerShell and built Entrance, a project allocation system on .NET Entity Framework.

I hold a Master of Computer Applications from S.R.M University and a Master of Science in Software Systems from Sri Krishna Arts and Science College.
